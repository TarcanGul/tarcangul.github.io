# Personal blog writing preferences

These instructions apply when drafting or editing posts under `src/pages/blogs/`.

- Treat text inside double parentheses, `((...))`, as an editorial instruction from the author, not publishable prose. When asked to revise or complete a draft, carry out the instruction and replace the note with the resulting content. Notes may request an explanation, fact check, link, or missing detail.
- Preserve the author's personal, first-person voice and creative intent. Do not invent experiences or technical details.
- Keep the writing concise and to the point. Use plain language and explain unfamiliar technical terms for musicians who may not use FL Studio.
- Do not use em dashes.
- Avoid the rhetorical rule of three: do not force ideas into three-part lists or parallel phrases for effect. Necessary factual lists are fine.
- Aim for a total reading time of 5 to 10 minutes, roughly 1,000 to 2,000 words. Do not pad the post to reach a word count.
- Verify factual claims and technical explanations against reliable sources, preferring official documentation. Include relevant links in the References section.
- Use confirmed links and details from the draft or accompanying notes. If required information is missing, ask the author rather than inventing it.
- Preserve existing frontmatter and section structure unless the requested edit calls for changes.
